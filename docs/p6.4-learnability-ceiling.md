# P6.4 — Learnability ceiling on Tier 4 (open scope)

**Date:** 2026-10-16 · **World:** `tier4-v2`, detected store `p5-7-live-2/base` ·
**Scope:** train+tune labels only; the sealed test split is untouched. ·
**Pre-registration:** `docs/decisions.md` row P6-4 (written, and scorer frozen, before any label
was read for it). **Not a claim.**

## Question

Every method so far is near chance on Tier 4: the detector, prompted Qwen (P6.1), Jev (P5.4)
and Nimble (P6.3). None of them *learned* the labels. A supervised ceiling separates two
explanations: "the labels are not recoverable from the label-free evidence" (fix the world or
brief), and "the signal is there and our methods miss it" (retune, fine-tune).

## Method

- **Units:** all 1,309 open evaluation units (caught and allowed); 202 abusive / 1,051
  legitimate in the readout, 56 ambiguous excluded. Abuse comes from 22 source groups.
- **Features:** 64 label-free features (`scripts/p6_4_features.py`, frozen in
  `data/tier4-v2/runs/p6-4-ceiling/manifest.json`). They are v2-render summaries, temporal claim
  summaries and the 12 F-* raw values. Per-account activity matched `render_unit` v2 on every unit.
  The detector score is a comparator, not a feature.
- **Models (fixed, untuned):** L2 logistic regression (C = 1, standardized) and
  `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` with defaults, using scikit-learn 1.9.1.
- **Validation:** `StratifiedGroupKFold`, 5 folds by source group, seed 20261016, scored once
  out of fold (`scripts/p6_4_ceiling.py`). Each fold held 2–6 abusive source groups.

## Reading

1. **The labels are only weakly recoverable from this evidence.** A supervised model trained
   on the labels reaches about 0.67 AUC by source-group CV. Its precision at matched volume
   rises from 0.18 to 0.20, against a 0.16 base rate. That falls short of the "method problem"
   bar, so the near-chance results so far are mostly a property of the world (look-alike
   evidence by design), not only of our methods.
2. **The signal there is family-specific, and it differs from the detector's.** The learned
   models catch most *Direct allocation cycling*, the family that holds 53 of the detector's
   60 misses (P6.0). They lose *Staged household*, which the detector's rules already catch.
   The two scores are complementary, which is the recall-layer premise. On this world, though,
   it rests on a handful of source groups.
3. **The zero-shot LLMs left signal on the table in the allowed multi-account pool.** Untuned
   linear features reach 0.76–0.79 there, against Nimble's 0.50–0.58 and the detector score's 0.68.
   That pool has only 4 abusive source groups, so this is a pointer, not a measurement.
4. **The estimate is unstable.** Per-fold AUC ranges from 0.55 to 0.86 with 2–6 abusive groups
   per fold. Any single number here carries wide, unquantified uncertainty.
